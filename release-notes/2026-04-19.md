    title: New tasks snap to weekdays
    body: Generated tasks now schedule onto Mon–Fri only. If there are no weekdays left in the current month, the planner offers next month instead.

    title: Simpler logging notes
    body: Logging progress on a task is one text field now — less to fill out so you're more likely to actually do it.
  - kind: improved
    title: Redesigned goals page
    body: Cleaner cards, clearer status, less visual clutter when you're scanning your list.
  - kind: fixed
    title: AI-generated goals no longer 400 on save
